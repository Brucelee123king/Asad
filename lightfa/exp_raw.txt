I want to share my experience of the Pakistan Navy PNCT Initial Test
conducted at PN Recruitment & Selection Center, Lahore. My initial test started
with the Intelligence portion. There were 75 MCQs to be completed in 30 minutes,
comprising both Verbal and Non-Verbal. In my case, both were almost equal, around
35 Non-Verbal and 40 Verbal. The Non-Verbal portion was quite easy. Most of the
questions were based on Clockwise / Anti-Clockwise rotation and Find the Odd One
Out. If attempted with focus, it was easily solvable. The Verbal portion was also
easy overall, but some parts were tricky. Analogies were a bit difficult due to
vocabulary / word meanings. There were questions where two words were synonyms and
we had to find the synonym for the third word from the options. The ABC series
(like ABC, DEF pattern) was confusing due to different letter arrangements. I
completed the intelligence portion 40 seconds before time. After that, the
Academic portion started. It consisted of 40 MCQs in 18 minutes. Compared to
Intelligence, Academic was slightly difficult. It was mostly English, followed by
Physics. Maths was very limited, only 2-3 MCQs, from Sequence & Series (Convergent
and Divergent). In English, prepositions were dominant, along with Adverbs and
Parts of Speech. There were 4-5 questions where a word was underlined in a
sentence and we had to identify whether it is an adverb, article, or preposition.
From General Knowledge, there was one MCQ about when the Eiffel Tower was built.
There were one or two more GK questions but I don't recall them exactly. No
question came from Islamiat or cricket. I completed the Academic portion 2 minutes
before time. Overall it was easy, but it requires good preparation as many
candidates failed in it. Out of around 80 students, many passed Intelligence but
failed in Academic. After clearing the test, they stamped my roll number slip and
informed that if I get shortlisted on merit, I will be called for further medical,
otherwise not. They will prepare a merit list based on marks and only top students
will be called for medical.
