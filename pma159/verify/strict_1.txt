[Sheet Q1] Orange Line train is working in which city?
    OPTIONS: Lahore | Karachi | Islamabad | Rawalpindi
    OUR ANSWER: Lahore
    OUR REASON: The Orange Line Metro Train runs in Lahore.
    OUR RELATED FACT: It is Pakistan's first metro train, opened in 2020, built with Chinese cooperation under CPEC.


[Sheet Q2] Which article of the Constitution of Pakistan gives freedom of speech?
    OPTIONS: Article 17 | Article 19 | Article 25 | Article 14
    OUR ANSWER: Article 19
    OUR REASON: Article 19 guarantees freedom of speech, subject to reasonable restrictions.
    OUR RELATED FACT: Article 19-A gives the right to information.


[Sheet Q3] Which article of the Constitution of Pakistan gives the right to education?
    OPTIONS: Article 19-A | Article 22 | Article 25-A | Article 37
    OUR ANSWER: Article 25-A
    OUR REASON: Article 25-A makes free and compulsory education a right for children aged 5 to 16.
    OUR RELATED FACT: It was added by the 18th Amendment in 2010.


[Sheet Q4] Which one of these is a greenhouse gas?
    OPTIONS: Oxygen | Nitrogen | Argon | CO2 (carbon dioxide)
    OUR ANSWER: CO2 (carbon dioxide)
    OUR REASON: Carbon dioxide traps heat in the atmosphere, so it is a greenhouse gas.
    OUR RELATED FACT: Methane, nitrous oxide and water vapour are other greenhouse gases.
