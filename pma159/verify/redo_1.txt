[Sheet Q22] Which is the largest river of the world by volume of water?
    OPTIONS: Amazon | Nile | Yangtze | Mississippi
    OUR ANSWER: Amazon
    OUR REASON: The Amazon carries more water than any other river.
    OUR RELATED FACT: By length the Nile and Amazon are close, and the Nile is traditionally called longest.



[Sheet Q23] Which is the smallest bird in the world?
    OPTIONS: Sparrow | Hummingbird (bee hummingbird) | Kiwi | Robin
    OUR ANSWER: Hummingbird (bee hummingbird)
    OUR REASON: The bee hummingbird of Cuba is the smallest bird, about 5 to 6 cm long.
    OUR RELATED FACT: Hummingbirds are the only birds able to fly backwards.
