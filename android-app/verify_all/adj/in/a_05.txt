#1394: (Forces Academy Test 1 · Q54) Which is different in the following: Ounce, Pound, Ton, Maund?
   A. Ounce
   B. Pound
   C. Ton
   D. Maund
   MARKED: D
   BLIND CHECKER: X (low) all are units of weight; no clear odd one
   REASON SHOWN: Ounce, pound and ton belong to the same Western (imperial) system, while the maund is a traditional South Asian unit.
   RELATED: Tip: a maund is about 40 kg in Pakistan today (37.3 kg in the older system).

#1731: (Forces Academy Test 12 · Q18) Which letter comes next: B, D, G, L, ?
   A. T
   B. S
   C. U
   D. V
   MARKED: C
   BLIND CHECKER: A (medium) differences 2,3,5,8
   REASON SHOWN: Positions are 2, 4, 7, 12 with differences 2, 3, 5, and each difference is one more than a power of 2 (2^0+1, 2^1+1 and so on: 2, 3, 5, 9). Next difference is 9: 12 + 9 = 21, which is U. Another reading of the gaps (2, 3, 5, 8) would give T, but the paper's answer key is U.
   RELATED: Tip: if differences are not simple, subtract a constant from them and look for powers of 2.
