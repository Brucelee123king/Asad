#1798: (Forces Academy Test 15 · Q3) Which one of the following is different? A. PQO, B. KJL, C. UYW, D. SRT, E. FEG
   A. PQO
   B. KJL
   C. UYW
   D. SRT
   E. FEG
   MARKED: A
   BLIND CHECKER: X (low) PQO and UYW both break the pattern
   REASON SHOWN: KJL, SRT and FEG each go one step back then two steps forward (-1, +2). PQO goes forward then back (+1, -2), the reverse of that pattern, which is the answer given in the key.
   RELATED: Tip: write the step sizes between neighbouring letters, as in +1, -2, to spot the odd group.
