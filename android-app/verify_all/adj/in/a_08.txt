#4057: If the 24th day of a month is Tuesday, what day will the 3rd be?
   A. Tuesday
   B. Friday
   C. Saturday
   D. Sunday
   MARKED: A
   BLIND CHECKER: X (low) ambiguous: same month gives Tuesday (A), next month of 31 days gives Friday (B)
   REASON SHOWN: From the 3rd back to the 24th is 21 days. 21 days is 3 full weeks plus 0 day(s), so the day is 0 day(s) before Tuesday, which is Tuesday.
   RELATED: Dates that are 7 days apart always fall on the same weekday, so only the remainder after dividing the gap by 7 matters.

#4689: What is the integral of tan x with respect to x?
   A. sec squared x + C
   B. ln |cos x| + C
   C. ln |sec x| + C
   D. -ln |sec x| + C
   MARKED: C
   BLIND CHECKER: D (high) 
   REASON SHOWN: Write tan x as sin x over cos x and put u = cos x; the integral becomes -ln |cos x| + C, which equals ln |sec x| + C.
   RELATED: The derivative of tan x is sec squared x.

#4830: What comes next in the series: 3x+4, 4x+2, ?
   A. 5x
   B. 5x+1
   C. 5x+2
   D. 6x
   MARKED: A
   BLIND CHECKER: A (low) pattern ambiguous; coeff +1, const -2 gives 5x
   REASON SHOWN: The coefficient of x increases by 1 each time (3, 4, 5) and the constant decreases by 2 (4, 2, 0). So the next term is 5x+0 = 5x.
   RELATED: Such a pattern is two arithmetic progressions running together.
