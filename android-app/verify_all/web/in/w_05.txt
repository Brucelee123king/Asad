#1841: (Forces Academy Test 16 · Q17) Which one of the following is different? A. Camel, B. Ox, C. Horse, D. Donkey, E. Mule
   A. Camel
   B. Ox
   C. Horse
   D. Donkey
   E. Mule
   MARKED: E
   EARLIER CHECKS: blind=X (low) Camel/Ox (ruminants) vs Horse/Donkey/Mule (equines); no unique odd one | adjudication=KEEP Mule is a hybrid, the others are species; weak question, but the key is defensible.
   REASON SHOWN: Camel, ox, horse and donkey are natural animal species. A mule is a hybrid, the offspring of a male donkey and a female horse.
